Tolkien Elvish to English Translation & Pronunciation Guide

J.R.R. Tolkien constructed multiple distinct Elvish languages over his lifetime. When using an English to Elvish translator for D&D or LOTR lore, it is essential to understand the two main dialects:

  • Sindarin (Common Elvish): The daily spoken tongue of the Elves of Middle-earth during the Third Age (spoken by Legolas, Arwen, and Elrond).
  • Quenya (High Elvish): The high ceremonial language of Valinor, used for poetry, sacred vows, and cosmic lore (similar to Latin in medieval Europe).
